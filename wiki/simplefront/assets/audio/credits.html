Adaptations

The recordings were converted to mono PCM WAV at 24 kHz, high-pass filtered at 65 Hz and peak-limited. Playback uses excerpts, gentle fades, stereo positioning and small speed variations. No endorsement by the recordists or the British Library is implied.
